
## Addendum, evening of 5 Nov: methodology figures, clock period note, synthesis table

Goal: close the Design Methodology and Synthesis gaps against the course marking sheet (Objective 5, Design Methodology 5, Implementation 10, Simulation and Synthesis 5, Presentation and Viva 5, Report 5, IEEE paper format 5).

**Added (commit 37d16bc, pushed to origin/main)**
- `docs/figures/block_diagram.png`: block diagram drawn from `rtl/mmio_top.v`.

- `docs/figures/synth_*`: Yosys schematics. RTL view of `mmio_top` 8 x 1, and gate-level sky130 views of the cells driving `rdata[0]` (core 8 x 1) and `err` (bus 8 x 1).

**Findings from the synthesis table**
- Flop count matches DEPTH x 16 (core) and DEPTH x 16 + 40 (bus) on all 24 runs.
- Flops are 41% to 66% of synthesis area. The share falls as read ports are added.
- Area grows 15% to 36% from synthesis to the end of the flow.
- Up to 16 entries the bus overhead after synthesis matches the end of the flow. From 32 entries on it does not: bus 32 x 4 adds -14 um2 after synthesis and 4,272 um2 at the end. My reading is that placement and timing repair cause most of the overhead at large sizes. Not checked against per-stage reports yet.
